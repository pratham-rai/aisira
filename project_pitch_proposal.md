## 2. THE VISION: A NEW DIGITAL ERA FOR YAKSHAGANA DISCOVERY
During a day-long mega-event like the Patla Sambhrama, multiple troupes perform, senior artists are honored, and thousands of devotees move across the grounds. Finding daily schedules, tracking specific *Prasangams*, and getting geolocated routes to Melas during the traveling season can be challenging.

**Aisira** is built to solve these logistical bottlenecks:
*   Devotees scan a simple QR code on the grounds to instantly load the day-long Sambhrama schedule.
*   They can save the platform as an installable app on their home screen (**PWA**) with a single tap, keeping all schedules and map locations **fully accessible offline** when networks are congested on the crowded grounds.
*   Devotees can subscribe to receive automated **WhatsApp alerts** sent directly to their phones exactly **1 hour** before a specific play or the Valedictory stage program begins.

## 4. PRODUCT VALUE FOR THE PATLA SAMBHRAMA & DEVOTEES

### 4.1. Instant 1-Hour WhatsApp Start Alerts
Devotees frequently miss the start times of their favorite *Prasangams* (plays) or the valedictory stage announcements. By tapping **💬 WhatsApp Alert** next to any segment on their phones, Aisira will automatically send them a highly-formatted, polite notification directly in their personal WhatsApp chats exactly 1 hour prior to start.

### 4.2. Pinch-to-Zoom Pamphlets & Artist Portals
The Sambhrama features highly detailed printed booklets detailing sponsors, foundation achievements, and artist schedules. Aisira showcases these in an interactive sliding carousel with a high-definition lightbox overlay, allowing users to pinch-and-zoom up to 300% on their mobile screens.

### 4.3. Mobile-First Offline Map & Caching (PWA)
With tens of thousands of devotees packed into the Sambhrama grounds, local mobile network towers (Jio, Airtel) inevitably congest and fail. Aisira's offline PWA caching guarantees that devotees can open, search, and navigate the entire event directory and map **completely offline** with zero loading lag.

### 4.4. 0%-Fee Direct UPI Devotional Support
Aisira houses a direct donation portal. Devotees and global NRIs can directly support the Yakshadruva Patla Foundation's welfare funds (medical, housing, housing funds) via a integrated UPI gateway. **Aisira charges 0% platform fees**—100% of all contributions go directly from the devotee to the Patla Foundation’s official bank account.
